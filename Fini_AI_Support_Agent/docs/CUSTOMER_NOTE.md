Subject: Paylane support agent — what ships next week, and what we need from you

Hi,

Here’s a plain-language summary of the support agent we’re putting behind Paylane.

**What it does**
- Verifies the customer with the email on file plus the last 4 digits of their card before talking about account details.
- Looks up transactions and, when policy allows, issues a full refund (settled charges only, $75 or less, less than 30 days old, customer already verified with Paylane, and not already refunded).
- Explains when a card is frozen using the reason we have on record.
- Hands off to your team with a ticket when something is outside those rules, or when the customer asks for a person.

**What it won’t do**
- Unfreeze cards.
- Partial refunds, refunds on pending charges, or refunding the same charge twice.
- Stretch the rules because someone is upset or claims special access (e.g. a spouse without verification).
- Share full card numbers or read back the email on file.
- Promise timelines or legal outcomes.

**What I’d want from you before launch**
1. Confirm the “exactly 30 days” cutover (we treat day 30 as escalate, not auto-refund) and the $75 ceiling.
2. Where freeze reasons come from, and what customers should hear when the reason field is empty.
3. How you want pending charges handled today (we can’t void them in this version — escalate vs. “wait for settle” wording).
4. Fraud playbook: who owns incidents over $500, and what the bot should say in the first reply.
5. A short tone sample from your best agents, plus the escalation queue / SLA customers should expect.
6. A few real (redacted) tickets that went wrong historically, so we can add them to the eval set.

Happy to jump on a call before go-live.
